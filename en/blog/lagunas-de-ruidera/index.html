Country house near the Lagunas de Ruidera

Turquoise lakes linked by waterfalls, for a day of relaxation or adventure.

The Lagunas de Ruidera are about 50 minutes by car from Casa Rural Eufemia. This natural park is a chain of lakes linked by small waterfalls, with turquoise water surrounded by pine forests.

Relax or adventure: your choice

  • Relax: spend the day by the water, with food, drinks and plenty of shade.
  • Hiking: trails with viewpoints over the lakes, including easy ones.
  • On the water: kayaking and paddle surfing.
  • Cycling: signposted tracks between the lakes.
  • Swimming: designated bathing areas in summer.

With children

It is a great family day out: clear water, space to play and walks for everyone. You do not need to be a hiker to enjoy it.

Combine it with Peñarroya

On the way is Peñarroya Castle, in Argamasilla de Alba, with free guided visits. Together they make a full day of nature and history.

And back home

After a day at the lakes, there is nothing like returning to Arenales de San Gregorio for a dip in the private pool and dinner in the courtyard.
