Don Quixote route: Cueva de Medrano and Peñarroya Castle

Argamasilla de Alba, where legend has it Cervantes began to imagine Don Quixote.

Argamasilla de Alba is about 20 km from Casa Rural Eufemia. It is a must on the Don Quixote route: here is the Cueva de Medrano and, close by, Peñarroya Castle above the reservoir.

The Cueva de Medrano

According to tradition, Miguel de Cervantes was imprisoned in the Cueva de Medrano, and this is where he began to imagine Don Quixote. From the outside it is a simple building, and it is not a big or overwhelming museum: it is a short, pleasant visit, perfect for a quiet morning or afternoon. You do not need to be a literature expert to enjoy it.

Check opening times and ticket prices before you go.

Peñarroya Castle

On a rock by the Peñarroya reservoir stands this medieval castle, with an interpretation centre and beautiful views over the water. It is the gateway to the Lagunas de Ruidera Natural Park.

Silvia's tipThere are free guided visits from Wednesday to Sunday and on bank holidays, showing the castle and its interpretation centre. They are run by the Fundación Cadisla together with Argamasilla de Alba Town Council, a foundation that employs people with disabilities. Book by phone between 12 noon and 2 pm on +34 926 501 263.

A full-day plan

Visit the Cueva de Medrano in the morning, go up to Peñarroya Castle and, if you fancy some nature, continue to the Lagunas de Ruidera, which are right next door.
