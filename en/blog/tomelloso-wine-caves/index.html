Tomelloso: wine, art, caves and great food

Virgen de las Viñas winery, centuries-old caves, distilleries and Michelin-starred food.

Tomelloso is 15-20 minutes by car from Casa Rural Eufemia. It is wine country: thousands of caves were dug under its streets to make and store wine, and today it is one of the best wine-tourism destinations in La Mancha.

Virgen de las Viñas winery

The Virgen de las Viñas cooperative brings together wine, art and tradition. On a guided visit you can see:

  • El Lagar ethnographic museum: the winery's old central building, with the vineyard tools used by Tomelloso's first settlers.
  • Centuries-old caves: dug into limestone and restored, they show how wine was made and stored around 100 years ago.
  • Infanta Elena Museum of Contemporary Art: works by Spanish and international artists inside the winery.

The visit includes a wine tasting. Book in advance and check times and prices directly with the winery.

The wine caves of Tomelloso

Under the houses of Tomelloso there are thousands of caves dug by hand, full of clay jars several metres tall. Many are privately owned and can be visited by appointment: going down into one is a glimpse into the town's history, often told by the family itself.

The Cuevas de Tomelloso website lists the caves open to visitors, such as Segundo Dios, Orígenes (the largest, with room for 70 people), Garcilaso, Galileo, Perales, Abuelo Moreno and Abuelo Patricio. It also offers a guided route through several caves in one morning.

The easiest option: Cueva Galileo (Calle Galileo, 8), dating from 1845, opens every day, including Saturday and Sunday mornings. Guided visits start at €4, with a vermouth or mistela tasting or with Manchego cheese, and children under 8 go free. Book on their website or by phone or WhatsApp on +34 608 670 160, and pay on the day.

Altosa and the distillery chimneys

Tomelloso is also a town of distilleries: their tall brick chimneys are part of the skyline. The one at Altosa is the only one still in operation. Altosa is one of Europe's major wine-alcohol distilleries, producing brandy and spirits, and Bodegas Verum belongs to the same group. Its caves are impressive.

How to visit: visits are arranged through Bodegas Verum and include the facilities, the ageing cave, the distillery with its still room and a tasting. They take place on Saturday mornings, last about two hours and always require advance booking: via their website form, at visitas@bodegasverum.com or by phone on +34 926 511 404 or +34 926 511 408 (Monday to Friday, 7 am to 3 pm).

Silvia's tipIf you come for a weekend, book the Saturday visit well in advance, as places are confirmed by phone or email. It is one of the most special visits in Tomelloso.

Museo del Carro and the bombos

The bombos are dry-stone huts that farmers built next to the vineyards. One of them houses the Museo del Carro y Aperos de Labranza (cart and farm tools museum), perfect for understanding rural life in La Mancha.

Where to eat in Tomelloso

  • Traditional La Mancha food: gachas, galianos, Manchego cheese and local wine in the town's traditional restaurants.
  • Epílogo: a Michelin-starred restaurant reinventing La Mancha cuisine.
  • Marquinetti: the trattoria of Jesús Marquina, several times world pizza champion.
