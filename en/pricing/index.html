Prices and booking

The whole house for up to 8 guests, from €180 per night. Check the rates and calendar on Airbnb and contact us: if you book directly, we will improve the price whenever we can.

Rates and conditions

  • Price: from €180 per night, whole house.
  • High season: June, July, August and September.
  • Low season: October to May, except bank holidays.
  • Minimum stay: 2 nights.
  • Check-in: from 4 pm. Check-out: before 11 am.
  • Long stays: weekly and monthly discounts in low season.
  • Pets: on request. Free of charge when booking directly.
  • Baby cot: free on request.
  • Parties and events: not allowed.
