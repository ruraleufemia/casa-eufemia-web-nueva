Photo gallery of Casa Rural Eufemia

Explore the house before you arrive: the living room with its fireplace, the bedrooms, the private pool, the courtyard with barbecue and the corners of La Mancha waiting for you nearby.

Living room, dining room and kitchen

Traditional La Mancha charm in every corner: wooden beams, tiled walls and a fully equipped kitchen.

Bedrooms and bathrooms

Three bedrooms for up to 8 guests, bedrooms 1 and 2 connected, and two bathrooms with a walk-in shower indoors.

Pool, courtyard and barbecue

A private pool for your exclusive use, a courtyard with trees and roses, and a porch with a barbecue and wood-fired oven.

The village: Arenales de San Gregorio

The calm of a La Mancha village, with the pine park and its playground very close to the house.

Things to do nearby

Windmills, lakes, Peñarroya Castle, the Cueva de Medrano and the Tomelloso wine caves: the best of La Mancha, a short drive away.
