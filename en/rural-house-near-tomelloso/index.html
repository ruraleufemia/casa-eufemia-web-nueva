Country house near Tomelloso, with pool and barbecue

A 15-20 minute drive from Tomelloso, wine and cave country. A whole house for 8 guests, with a private pool.

Casa Rural Eufemia is in Arenales de San Gregorio, a 15-20 minute drive from Tomelloso. Tomelloso is wine and cave country, and our house is a comfortable base for groups of friends and families who want tastings, winery visits and good food, with a pool and barbecue to come home to.

Why stay here if you are visiting Tomelloso

  • A whole house for your group: 290 m² on one floor, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and up to 8 guests.
  • Fully equipped kitchen and a dining room with a fireplace for long after-dinner conversations.
  • Private pool, a patio with stone tables and a barbecue with wood-fired oven.
  • Free parking right at the door.
  • A house for quiet groups: families and friends who come to relax and enjoy themselves, not to party.

What to do in Tomelloso

Under the streets of Tomelloso lie thousands of caves dug out to make and store wine, and it is one of the best wine-tourism destinations in La Mancha. You can visit the Virgen de las Viñas winery and olive-oil mill, go down into a centuries-old cave among clay jars, or see the distilleries and the dry-stone bombos. Details and how to book are in our Tomelloso guide.

And 10 minutes from the house you have the windmills of Campo de Criptana, perfect for sunset.

Silvia's tipI know people at the wineries. Ask us about cave visits and, if you stay 5 nights or more and book directly, a guided cave visit can be your free experience.

Questions about staying near Tomelloso

How far is the house from Tomelloso?

About a 15-20 minute drive. We are in Arenales de San Gregorio (Ciudad Real).

Can you help me organise cave and winery visits?

Yes. We advise you on what to visit and how to book, and if you stay 5 nights or more and book directly, you can choose a guided cave visit as your free experience.

Is there a pool and a barbecue?

Yes. The pool is private, for the exclusive use of our guests, and there is a barbecue and wood-fired oven under a covered porch.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, on request: one or two small or calm pets. No extra charge if you book directly with us.

What is the minimum stay and what are the check-in times?

Minimum stay is 2 nights. Check-in from 16:00 and check-out before 11:00.
