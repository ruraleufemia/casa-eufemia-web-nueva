The house

Traditional La Mancha charm with every comfort

Living and dining room with a fireplace, a fully equipped kitchen, three bedrooms and two bathrooms, all on one floor and ready for 8 guests. Heat-pump air conditioning in the dining room and main bedroom, a pellet stove, fast free Wi-Fi, TV and board games for family evenings.

About us

Hello, I'm Silvia (and this is Maya)

Casa Rural Eufemia is named after my grandmother. I grew up here every summer and weekend with my grandparents. The street is named after my great-grandfather, Juan José Jiménez, and my mother grew up here. These walls hold my family's history.

When my parents passed away, it was the typical village house destined to be sold or to fall apart. I did not want to lose my childhood memories, so we took the plunge: new roofs, and more than a year in which I restored the furniture, the beams and every crack with my own hands.

Today I open it with great excitement so that other families can enjoy it as I did. It is made for families and groups of friends who get along and come to enjoy: not a party house, but a peaceful place to rest, share and make memories. My husband and I will be delighted to help you discover La Mancha.

And Maya, my two-year-old princess, is the joy of the house. She arrived at a difficult time and changed everything. She will probably come to say hello.

The village

Arenales de San Gregorio, a peaceful La Mancha village

The calm of a traditional village, where days go by slowly and children still play in the street.

Very close to the house is Los Pinares, the village pine park: lawns, shade, a playground and the pool bar, with huge sandwiches at good prices and lovely sunsets. Ideal in summer while the kids play and the grown-ups have a drink in the cool air.

Frequently asked questions

Does the house have a barbecue?

Yes. There is a covered porch with a built-in barbecue and a wood-fired oven, plus stone tables in the courtyard.

Is it suitable for guests with reduced mobility?

Yes, for guests who walk with a walker or a cane. The whole house is on one floor with no steps, there is step-free access through the back door and a walk-in shower. It is not suitable for wheelchairs, as the interior doors are not wide enough.

How many guests can stay and what are the beds?

Up to 8 guests, in 3 bedrooms with 2 bathrooms. Bedroom 1: a 135 cm double bed and a 140 cm sofa bed. Bedroom 2: two 105 cm beds and a 90 cm extra bed. Bedroom 3: a 105 cm bed. Plus another 90 cm extra bed in the dining room. Bedrooms 1 and 2 are connected, ideal for families.

Is there heating?

Yes. Heat-pump air conditioning in the dining room and main bedroom, a pellet stove in the hallway, a fireplace in the living room and a portable electric heater.

Is there Wi-Fi? Can I work remotely?

Yes, fast and free Wi-Fi, perfect for remote work. From October to May (except bank holidays) we offer weekly and monthly discounts: just ask.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, on request: one or two small or calm pets, no potentially dangerous breeds. Booking directly with us, your pet stays free of charge.

Do you have a baby cot?

Yes, a cot is available free of charge. Just ask when you book.

What are the free La Mancha experiences?

When you book directly with us for 5 nights or more, you choose one free experience (two for 10 nights): La Mancha cooking, lamb from the wood-fired oven, a wine tasting, a guided visit to a Tomelloso wine cave, the grape or saffron harvest, or a bike ride through the vineyards. Depending on the season and availability.

What is the minimum stay and check-in time?

Minimum 2 nights. Check-in from 4 pm and check-out before 11 am. If you book directly and it is available, you can arrive earlier and leave later.

Where can I park?

Right outside the door, free of charge. The house faces two streets and there is always space a few metres away.

Can we hold parties?

No. Casa Rural Eufemia is made for families and groups of friends who get along and come to enjoy a quiet stay. Parties and events are not allowed.

Do you help us plan our visit?

Yes, gladly. Tell us what you would like and we will prepare a plan: windmills, caves, wineries, nature or a tapas route. When we can, we come along in person; otherwise we give you a dossier with where to go, what to see and where to eat.
